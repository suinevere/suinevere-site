Out Now!



Finally, the raw 32-bit horsepower of the Sega Saturn is put to proper use: displaying crisp, perfectly rendered text. Over thirty one classic Infocom adventures are packed onto a single disc, complete with full Sega Saturn Keyboard support so your fingers can keep up with your imagination. And thanks to NetLink, anyone with a modem can now drag three friends into Zork for four-player simultaneous multiplayer!
